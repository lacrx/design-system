# Overview

A warm, editorial design for a community advocacy site. The palette is rooted in burnt orange and cream, evoking printed matter and civic signage. The tone is welcoming and authoritative without being institutional.

# Colors

The palette centers on a warm orange spectrum from deep brown-black (`--text-primary`) to pale cream (`--bg-primary`). Neutrals carry a warm undertone to stay cohesive. High contrast between text and background; accent color is a bold burnt orange used sparingly on interactive elements.

# Typography

Display: Instrument Serif for headlines. It has the weight of a newspaper masthead without the stiffness. Body: Inter for all running text, nav, and UI. Clean and neutral, lets the display face carry the personality. Mono: JetBrains Mono for data tables and code if needed.

# Layout

Max content width: 720px for prose, 1080px for dashboards. Generous vertical spacing (--space-2xl between sections). Side gutters: 16px minimum. Cards use subtle borders, not shadows; the warm palette provides enough contrast.

# Components

Buttons: solid fill with `--accent-primary` background, `--text-inverse` text. No border radius beyond `--radius-sm`. Links: `--text-accent` with underline on hover. Cards: `--bg-secondary` background, `--border-default` border, no shadow. Form inputs: transparent background, bottom border only.

# Do's and Don'ts

- DO: Use the warm palette consistently; every surface should feel like it belongs to the same family.
- DO: Let typography do the heavy lifting. Big headlines, comfortable line height.
- DON'T: Add cool blues or grays; they clash with the warm base.
- DON'T: Use heavy shadows or gradients. The design is flat and print-inspired.
